## 2. Competitive Context — read before designing anything

The lower Susquehanna already has an active catfish tournament scene, and **Long Level is not uncontested territory**:

- **Catfish Mafia Tournament Series** runs an 8-event trail on the Susquehanna from Sunbury down to the Conowingo pool, and is listed under the American Catfishing Association as *"Catfish Mafia Tournament Series – Long Level."* Contact: Andrew Lentz. Their branding is flat green/white with silhouetted angler figures — grassroots, one-screen, concept-driven.
- **Cabela's / Bass Pro King Kat Tournament Trail** — the national benchmark. Navy-and-gold athletic crest, crowned photorealistic catfish, layered outlines.
- **Twisted Cat Outdoors** — chrome gradients, blue oval, fish bursting the frame. Boat-wrap aesthetic.
- **American Catfishing Association** — heavy condensed caps with barbels curled into the letterform counters. The most design-literate mark in the category.
- **Eric Altemus Memorial Catfish Tournament** (PA) — Wix-built, photo-led, straightforward.

**Strategic read:** the category is saturated with dimensional, gradient-heavy, navy-and-gold marks. Nearly everyone looks the same. A **flat, faded, heritage-print identity** differentiates immediately — *and* costs far less to screen print, because every gradient and outline in those competitor logos is another screen and another setup fee.

## 3. Tone of Voice

Rugged, authentic, competitive, welcoming. A working marina event, not a corporate regatta.

**Write like:** a confident local. Short sentences. Plain words. Specific and factual on logistics — anglers hate ambiguity. Competitive but welcoming; first-timers and 20-year regulars should both feel invited.

**Avoid:** corporate filler ("elevate your experience"), stacked fish puns, event-planner formality.

**Sample lines:**
- "125 a boat. Four anglers max. Biggest cat on the scale wins."
- "Scales open 4PM Saturday at Lake Clarke Marina. Don't be late."

## 4. Visual Direction — Vintage / Heritage River-Town

The reference points are **not** other fishing tournaments. They're:

- 1950s tackle shop signage and hand-painted marina signs
- Vintage fishing lure box labels and tackle packaging
- Old embroidered scout/club patches
- Faded screen-printed souvenir shirts
- National park and WPA poster art

**Core characteristics:**
- **Flat.** No gradients, no chrome, no bevels, no drop shadows.
- **Limited ink.** Three colors maximum on any printed piece.
- **Faded.** Slightly desaturated, warm-shifted, as if sun-bleached.
- **Textured.** Subtle halftone dots and light distressing — worn, not destroyed.
- **Bold linework.** Confident, woodcut-like weight that survives at small sizes.

**Explicitly avoid:** the chrome/gradient/airbrush look of Twisted Cat, the crowned-mascot athletic crest of King Kat, and anything that reads as 2005 sports team.

## 5. Color Palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Susquehanna Slate** (primary) | `#2E4A52` | 46, 74, 82 | 44, 10, 0, 68 | Primary brand color — logo, headers, dark sections |
| **Rust Red** (accent) | `#A8442A` | 168, 68, 42 | 0, 60, 75, 34 | CTAs, buttons, highlights, "Register Now" |
| **Aged Ochre** (accent) | `#C68B2C` | 198, 139, 44 | 0, 30, 78, 22 | Banners, decorative rules, dark-background accents |
| **Moss Green** (secondary) | `#5A6B4A` | 90, 107, 74 | 16, 0, 31, 58 | Supporting accent, rules/info sections |
| **Walnut Brown** (secondary) | `#4A3728` | 74, 55, 40 | 0, 26, 46, 71 | Earthy support, borders, wood-tone elements |
| **Aged Oat** (light neutral) | `#EDE4D0` | 237, 228, 208 | 0, 4, 12, 7 | Page background, light text on dark |
| **River Ink** (dark neutral) | `#211D1A` | 33, 29, 26 | 0, 12, 21, 87 | Body text, one-color logo, dark backgrounds |

Note that the neutrals are **warm** — Aged Oat rather than white, River Ink rather than black. This is what produces the aged-paper feel. Never substitute pure `#FFFFFF` or `#000000`.

### Contrast — measured, not guessed

Ratios against **Aged Oat** `#EDE4D0` (the standard page background):

| Color | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| River Ink | 13.23:1 | ✅ Body text — AAA |
| Walnut Brown | 8.89:1 | ✅ Body text — AAA |
| Susquehanna Slate | 7.49:1 | ✅ Body text — AAA |
| Rust Red | 4.71:1 | ✅ Body text — AA |
| Moss Green | 4.57:1 | ✅ Body text — AA (marginal; prefer for large text) |
| **Aged Ochre** | **2.33:1** | ❌ **Fails. Decorative use only on light backgrounds** |

**Aged Ochre on River Ink is 5.68:1 — passes.** So ochre is a *dark-background* accent. Use it for banner text on slate or ink, never for text on oat or white.

### Usage rules
- **Backgrounds:** Aged Oat for standard sections; Susquehanna Slate or River Ink for dark/hero sections.
- **Text:** River Ink on light; Aged Oat on dark. Never body copy in Ochre, Moss, or Rust.
- **CTAs:** Rust Red fill with Aged Oat text. Reserve rust for actions — if everything is rust, nothing is.
- **Don't** pair Moss Green with Walnut Brown as text-on-background; both are mid-value and fail together.

### Dark mode
Susquehanna Slate and River Ink both work as dark backgrounds. On either, use Aged Oat for text and **Aged Ochre** for accents — ochre finally earns its keep on dark.

## 6. Typography

| Role | Font | Source | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** | [Alfa Slab One](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alfa+Slab+One) | Google Fonts | Logo wordmark, H1, hero headlines. Heavy vintage slab — the sign-painter voice. |
| **Sub-display / condensed** | [Staatliches](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Staatliches) | Google Fonts | Banner text, section labels, badge lettering, stat numbers. |
| **Accent script** | [Yellowtail](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Yellowtail) | Google Fonts | Sparingly — "Annual," "Est. 2027," pricing flourishes. Never body copy, never more than a few words. |
| **Body / UI** | [Libre Franklin](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Libre+Franklin) | Google Fonts | Paragraphs, nav, forms, buttons, footer. Neutral workhorse that lets the display carry the character. |

All four are free and already wired into `/website`.

### Hierarchy
- **H1** — Alfa Slab One, sentence-height, tight leading
- **H2/H3** — Staatliches uppercase, wide letter-spacing
- **H4 / labels** — Libre Franklin SemiBold uppercase, wide tracking, small
- **Body** — Libre Franklin Regular, River Ink on light / Aged Oat on dark
- **Buttons** — Staatliches uppercase, wide tracking
- **Accent** — Yellowtail, max ~3 words

## 7. Logo System

**Artwork is generated, not hand-drawn in code.** See `logos/IMAGE-GEN-PROMPTS.md` for the copy-paste prompts and the production workflow.

Required final files:

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `logo-primary.svg/png` | Full lockup — badge/mark + wordmark |
| `logo-horizontal.svg/png` | Mark left, wordmark right |
| `logo-stacked.svg/png` | Mark above wordmark |
| `icon-mark.svg/png` | Catfish mark alone — favicon, avatars, small use |
| `badge.svg/png` | Circular patch treatment |
| `logo-onecolor-slate.svg/png` | Single ink, Susquehanna Slate |
| `logo-onecolor-oat.svg/png` | Single ink, Aged Oat — for dark garments |

Every mark ships as **transparent PNG + SVG**.

### Clear space & minimum size
- Clear space on all sides equal to the height of the catfish's head in that lockup (~10% of total mark height). Nothing intrudes.
- Minimum: icon mark **24px / 0.25in**; full lockup **120px / 1in** wide.

### Do
- Use the approved color combinations as-is
- Use the one-color versions on busy photography and dark garments
- Keep the distressed texture — it's the point

### Don't
- Don't add gradients, chrome, bevels, or drop shadows
- Don't stretch or skew — scale proportionally
- Don't recolor outside the palette
- Don't place the full-color mark on a background it lacks contrast against — switch to one-color
- Don't rebuild the lockups into new arrangements
- Don't over-distress it into illegibility

## 8. Imagery Style

Real photography once available:
- Golden hour and early morning on the water
- Boats, rods, tackle, hands at the scale — working textures, not studio polish
- Weigh-in crowd energy at Lake Clarke Marina
- Wide Susquehanna river shots for hero backgrounds, behind a dark overlay

Grade everything **warm and slightly faded** — Kodak Portra, not saturated HDR. Consistent grading is what makes a photo set feel like one brand.

Until real photos exist, use flat Susquehanna Slate color blocks or the icon mark as an oversized watermark. Better an honest color block than a generic stock photo of the wrong lake.

## 9. Merchandise

See `merch-mockups/` for specs and `logos/IMAGE-GEN-PROMPTS.md` prompts 6–7.

**Standing rule: three inks maximum.** Every extra color is another screen and another setup charge, and the faded three-color look is more authentically vintage anyway.

- **Shirts** — heather oatmeal, heather slate, or washed rust. Avoid pure white and pure black; they fight the warm palette.
- **Placement** — badge on left chest, or full mark centered on the back with the year beneath.
- **Hats** — one-color embroidery of the icon mark, ~2.5in wide, front panel centered.
- **Stickers** — 3in circular badge, 4in die-cut fish.
