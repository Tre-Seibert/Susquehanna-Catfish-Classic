About & Rules

Everything you need to know before you launch. Read it once so nobody's arguing at the scale.

Overview

A working-marina tournament

The Susquehanna Catfish Classic is an annual two-day catfish tournament on the lower Susquehanna River, hosted by Lake Clarke Marina in Wrightsville, Pennsylvania. It runs the last Friday and Saturday of August, every year.

The format is simple on purpose. Pay your entry, launch when you want, fish the water you know, and bring your best fish to the scale by 4:00 PM Saturday. No qualifiers, no membership dues, no series points to chase. Show up and fish.

Flatheads and channel cats both run this stretch of river in late August, and the Long Level pool has produced serious fish for as long as anybody around here has been running trotlines. Whether you're a first-timer with a jon boat or you've been catting this river for thirty years, you're in the same tournament.

Official Rules

Tournament Rules

⚠️ Draft — pending committee approval

The sections below are placeholders reflecting the confirmed details. Final rules need sign-off from the tournament committee and Lake Clarke Marina before entries open.

1. Entry & Eligibility
  • Entry fee is $125 per boat, covering the full two days.
  • Maximum four (4) anglers per boat. No substitutions after check-in.
  • Every angler must hold a valid Pennsylvania fishing license where required by law.
  • Anglers under 16 fish free but must be counted toward the four-angler limit.
  • Entries close August 20 or when the field fills, whichever comes first.
2. Tournament Hours
  • Fishing opens Friday at 6:00 AM.
  • Lines must be out of the water by 3:00 PM Saturday.
  • Scales open 4:00 PM Saturday at Lake Clarke Marina and close at 6:00 PM sharp.
  • Boats arriving after the scales close will not be weighed. No exceptions.

Host & Weigh-In

Lake Clarke Marina

Lake Clarke Marina sits on the west shore of Lake Clarke in York County — a working marina on Long Level Road, Wrightsville. Host and weigh-in are both here.

Launch Friday morning, fish the pool, and bring your best fish back to the same dock. Scales open 4:00 PM Saturday.
