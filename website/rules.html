About & Rules

Everything you need to know before you launch. Read it once so nobody's arguing at the scale.

Overview

A working-marina tournament

The Susquehanna Catfish Classic is an annual two-day catfish tournament on the lower Susquehanna River, hosted by Lake Clarke Marina in Wrightsville, Pennsylvania. It runs the last Friday and Saturday of August, every year.

The format is simple on purpose. Check in Friday at 5:00 PM, fish the water you know through the night, and get your best fish to the scale Saturday afternoon before 7:00 PM. No qualifiers, no membership dues, no series points to chase. Show up and fish.

$1,000 goes to the heaviest catfish, with payouts down to third and a separate bracket for anglers 16 and under. Friday evening there's live music, a food truck, and raffles on the bank — you don't need a boat to come out.

Flatheads and channel cats both run this stretch of river in late August, and the Long Level pool has produced serious fish for as long as anybody around here has been running trotlines. Whether you're a first-timer with a jon boat or you've been catting this river for thirty years, you're in the same tournament.

Official Rules

Tournament Rules

⚠️ Sections 3, 4 and 5 still pending

Entry, hours and payouts are confirmed. Boundaries, legal fish & tackle, and the weigh-in procedure are still placeholders and need sign-off from the tournament committee and Lake Clarke Marina before entries open.

1. Entry & Eligibility
  • Entry fee is $200 per boat, covering the whole tournament.
  • Maximum four (4) anglers per boat. No substitutions after check-in.
  • Every angler must hold a valid Pennsylvania fishing license where required by law.
  • Anglers 16 and under fish free but must be counted toward the four-angler limit. They compete in their own Kids Bracket.
  • Registration must be completed no later than 48 hours before check-in — for 2027 that's Wednesday, August 25 at 5:00 PM — or when the field fills, whichever comes first.
  • No returns or cancellations. Once submitted, your entry fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.
2. Tournament Hours
  • Check-in is Friday, August 27 at 5:00 PM at Lake Clarke Marina. All boats must check in before fishing.
  • Lines go in after check-in — you fish straight through the night.
  • Scales open 4:00 PM Saturday at Lake Clarke Marina and close at 7:00 PM sharp.
  • Your fish must be at the scale before 7:00 PM. Boats arriving after the scales close will not be weighed. No exceptions.
3. Boundaries

Placeholder — define the fishable stretch of the Susquehanna, upstream and downstream limits, and any excluded or restricted zones. Committee to confirm.

4. Legal Fish & Tackle

Placeholder — species counted (flathead, channel, blue), rod limits, bait restrictions, and whether trotlines / jugs / limb lines are permitted. Committee to confirm.

5. Weigh-In Procedure
  • Weigh-in is at Lake Clarke Marina, 1552 Long Level Rd.
  • Fish must be alive and in reasonable condition to be weighed.
  • Placeholder — confirm number of fish weighed per boat, live-well requirements, dead-fish penalties, and catch-and-release policy.
6. Payouts & Awards
  • 1st Place — Heaviest Catfish: $1,000
  • 2nd Place — Heaviest Catfish: $250
  • 3rd Place — Heaviest Catfish: $125
  • Kids Bracket (16 & under): $125
  • Placings are decided by the single heaviest catfish weighed per boat.
  • Raffle prizes are drawn separately at the weigh-in and are not part of the tournament payout.
7. Safety & Conduct
  • Coast Guard–approved PFDs required for every person aboard.
  • All Pennsylvania boating regulations apply at all times.
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct, and any attempt to cheat, means disqualification.
  • Tournament officials' decisions are final.
  • The event runs rain or shine. Officials may suspend for lightning or dangerous water.

Host & Weigh-In

Lake Clarke Marina

Lake Clarke Marina sits on the west shore of Lake Clarke in York County — a working marina on Long Level Road, Wrightsville. Host and weigh-in are both here.

Check in Friday evening, fish the pool overnight, and bring your best fish back to the same dock. Scales run 4:00–7:00 PM Saturday.
