The Tournament

Two Days. One River. Big Cats.

The Susquehanna Catfish Classic runs out of Lake Clarke Marina on the last Friday and Saturday of August — every year, rain or shine. Lines hit the water Friday morning and the scales open at 4:00 PM Saturday, same marina.

It's open to anybody with a boat and a license. Bring a crew of up to four, fish the pool the way you know how, and bring your best fish to the scale. No qualifiers, no membership, no politics.
