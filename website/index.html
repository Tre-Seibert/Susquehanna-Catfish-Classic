The Tournament

One Night. One River. Big Cats.

The Susquehanna Catfish Classic runs out of Lake Clarke Marina on the last Friday and Saturday of August — every year, rain or shine. Check-in is 4:00 PM Friday, fishing starts at 7:00 PM, and you fish straight through the night. Scales open at 5:00 PM Saturday and close at 8:00.

It's open to anybody with a boat and a license. Bring a crew of up to four, fish the pool the way you know how, and bring your best fish to the scale. No qualifiers, no membership, no politics.
